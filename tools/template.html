        After you download

        1. Unzip the file. On Windows, right-click it and choose Extract All. You get a folder with fab-order.xml and a card-images folder inside.
        2. Get the Autofill program (one time only) from the MPC Autofill releases page. Download the file for your computer, e.g. autofill-windows.exe.
        3. Move the Autofill program into the unzipped folder, right next to fab-order.xml.
        4. Double-click the Autofill program. If Windows shows a blue "protected your PC" box, click More info then Run anyway. Choose fab-order.xml if it asks.
        5. Let it work. It opens Google Chrome and fills in the MakePlayingCards order for you. Don't click in that window until it says it has finished.
        6. Check and pay. Look over the cards on the MakePlayingCards page, then add to cart and check out yourself.
        Tips and answers
        • Click any card picture to see it bigger. Use Change art to pick a different printing, such as an extended or full art version.
        • Double-sided cards (Figments, Invokes, some promo heroes and more) get their back side printed automatically. Use Print without the back if you only want the front.
        • The art comes from Fabrary's card images, at about 550 × 760 pixels. That prints fine for playtesting, but it isn't as sharp as a real card.
        • This page adds the extra edge (bleed) MakePlayingCards needs, so nothing important gets cut off.
        • Your list clears if you close or reload this page, so download before you leave.
        • Google Chrome needs to be installed for the Autofill program to work.
